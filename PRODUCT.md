# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, prospective clients, and collaborators evaluating Raffa Rizqi's full-stack development work from phones, tablets, laptops, and large desktop displays. They need to understand his capabilities, inspect credible project evidence, and reach him quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present a credible, memorable portfolio for Raffa Rizqi Ramdani, demonstrate production-oriented frontend and full-stack work, and convert qualified visitors into hiring conversations or project inquiries. The RebaFlix route also provides a focused product download and installation flow.

## Brand Personality

Bold, direct, capable. The interface should feel handcrafted and technically confident, with a disciplined neo-brutalist visual language and clear Indonesian-first communication.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-generated landing pages, repetitive icon-card grids, excessive gradients or glass effects, oversized decorative shadows, vague placeholder claims, and desktop-first layouts that merely shrink on mobile.

## Design Principles

- Make project evidence the strongest visual proof.
- Preserve the neo-brutalist identity while keeping every interaction practical.
- Design mobile and touch states as first-class experiences.
- Keep calls to action obvious without overwhelming the portfolio narrative.
- Use motion to support orientation, never to block content or control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, browser zoom, reduced-motion preferences, readable contrast, comfortable touch targets, and layouts that remain usable from narrow mobile screens through large displays.
